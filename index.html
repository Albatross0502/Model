04 — The student process

From brief
to final piece.

A look into how I tackle design assignments, from the initial spark of inspiration to the sewing machine.

01 / Research

The Moodboard

Building the visual vocabulary for a project through historical research, art, and fabric sourcing.

02 / Sketch

The Concepts

Translating ideas to paper, refining silhouettes, and developing color stories for the collection.

03 / Pattern

The Architecture

Drafting flat patterns and draping on the mannequin to solve structural challenges before cutting final fabric.

04 / Construct

The Execution

Careful stitching, hand-finishing, and multiple fittings to ensure the garment matches the original vision.
